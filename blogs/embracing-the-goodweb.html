Embracing the Goodweb

October 13, 2026

This internet webpage is an attempt to bring the charm and personality back into the internet. Growing up I used to play around in iWeb designing my own ideal web space, but never could get to the "publishing" part. Probably for the better, there is no way to know what my eight year old self would have written into the annals of the internet archive.

Thanks for checking out my blog. This will be the first of (hopefully) many posts where I will discuss what I am reading, thinking, and feeling. Don't forget to bookmark my page. Hope to see you again soon!
